## What upstream did

Introduced renderer reconciliation so glass themes use xterm's DOM renderer while opaque
themes use WebGL, with cache state and theme transitions managing renderer replacement.

## Why it matters to us

The fork currently prefers Canvas for alpha compositing and falls back to WebGL. DOM
rendering is valuable as another strategy, not as an unconditional rewrite.

## Mapping into our tree

The renderer helper, cache state, view integration, and theme transitions map entirely to
`core/frontend/terminal/`.

## Seam feedback

No explicit `terminal.engine` selector exists. Adding one is warranted so DOM and the
current accelerated renderers remain testable variants behind one reconciliation point.
