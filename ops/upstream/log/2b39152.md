## What upstream did

Upgraded xterm and its addons to version 6-compatible releases and adjusted terminal
renderer, theme, CSS, and lockfile behavior for the new APIs.

## Why it matters to us

The fork remains on xterm 5.5. The upgrade is useful, but its frontend files have moved
and the fork also carries Canvas fallback and session-lifecycle work not present upstream.

## Mapping into our tree

Dependency changes remain rooted in `package.json`; renderer and theme changes map to
`core/frontend/terminal/`. Upstream TODO and vision edits are intake-only context.

## Seam feedback

The terminal renderer seam is the right boundary, but version migration must preserve the
fork's cached renderer and lifecycle contracts instead of replacing the component whole.
